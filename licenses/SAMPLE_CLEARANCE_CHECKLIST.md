## Required evidence (per sample)

- [ ] Written license or assignment granting commercial use in a software instrument
- [ ] Right to embed in binary distribution (VST3 bundle)
- [ ] No conflicting exclusive library deal
- [ ] Performer / session musician releases (if applicable)
- [ ] Master recording owner authorization

## User-imported samples

Users may load their own WAV/AIFF via `SampleLibrary`. The plugin does not verify user file rights — document in EULA that users are responsible for content they import.

## Placeholder policy

Current `Resources/Factory/*.wav` files are **synthetic placeholders** generated by `scripts/generate_factory_assets.py`. They are **not cleared for commercial use** until replaced and recorded in this checklist.
