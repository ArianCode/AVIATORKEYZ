# Governance, entity, equity, and money — questions for counsel

**Parties (working labels):** Arian (coding, product direction); Keyz (brand, industry, marketing, content).  
**Project:** AviatorKeyz (VST3 instrument / related product).

**Purpose:** Conversation starters and **checklist questions** for a first meeting. **Do not** treat answers below as decided until you have **signed** agreements. **This is not legal or tax advice.**

---

## 1. Entity and equity

- What **entity** should own the code, name, and revenue (LLC, corporation, UK/EU form), and in **which jurisdiction**?  
- **Equity split** — if any: what % for each founder, and is it **fixed** or “to be decided”? (Avoid hard-committing a split in a memo without signatures.)  
- **Vesting:** standard 4-year with 1-year cliff? Any **acceleration** on acquisition or on IP deliverables?  
- **Intellectual property:** Should **all** prior and future work on the product be **assigned to the company**? What about work done **before** company formation?  
- **Departure / buyback:** If one person leaves, what happens to **unvested** equity, **name**, **social accounts**, and **in-flight deals**?  
- **Disputes:** Mediation first, then arbitration — **where** and under **which** rules?  
- **Deadlock:** If Arian and Keyz disagree on a major decision (e.g. pricing, rebrand, exclusive partnership), who breaks the tie? Board? Independent third party? Roster of reserved matters?

---

## 2. Roles vs. compensation (Keyz in particular)

- Is Keyz a **co-founder** with **equity only**, a **revenue share** partner, a **contractor** (invoices, statement of work), or a **blended** arrangement?  
- If **contractor:** should counsel review **work-made-for-hire** / IP assignment in **Keyz’s jurisdiction**?  
- If Keyz is the **“face”** of the product: what **exclusivity** (if any), **name/likeness** release for ads, and **right to decline** a campaign?  
- **Content deliverables** (demos, posts): who **owns** the deliverables, and is there a **reversion** if the project ends?

---

## 3. IP ownership (tight coupling with [IP_SUMMARY.md](IP_SUMMARY.md))

- Do we need a **separate** IP assignment from each of Arian, Keyz, and any **contractors** (design, sample pack)?  
- For **factory samples and presets** — is a **provenance** spreadsheet enough for you, or do you need a **warranty** from contributors?  
- **Trademark:** timeline and budget for **search + application** for “AviatorKeyz” and logo.  
- **Pre-existing** code or design: **list + license in** to the company — form?

---

## 4. Money, tax, and payouts (high level; CPA for execution)

- **Who receives** marketplace payouts (personal vs entity, which bank account)? If **migrating** from personal to entity, how?  
- **Split of revenue** after **costs** (JUCE, ads, third-party services): % vs fixed fee vs hybrid?  
- **How often** is reconciliation (e.g. monthly) and in **what** form (spreadsheet, accounting software)?  
- **Tax forms** (U.S. 1099 / W-2 / K-1, or non-U.S. equivalents): who issues to whom, and for **which** payments?  
- **Nexus & VAT/GST** for digital sales: which counsel or firm handles **SaaS / plugin** sales in **EU, UK, U.S. states**?

---

## 5. What not to “answer” in writing without a lawyer

- Do **not** state a **fixed equity %** in email or a draft deck if you have **not** both signed.  
- Do **not** promise a **revenue share** to a third party without a **signed** agreement.  
- Do **not** assume JUCE/Steinberg/Apple compliance from **this repo alone** — **licenses and developer accounts** are **external**.
